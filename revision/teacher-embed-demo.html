Demo

What this demo is

This page shows how a teacher deck can open an embedded Google Form in a side panel, so an assessment is entered only in Google’s own form.

This is not the full private teacher deck. It contains no real lesson content, no answer guidance and no learner information. Every question and choice here is invented for testing.
  • The test form has one required choice and no text fields.
  • Please do not add any personal information anywhere.
  • Repeat submissions are recorded separately by Google; nothing here merges or removes them.
Invented prompt

Sorting invented blocks

Imagine a tray of invented toy blocks:

  • blue cube
  • blue cylinder
  • red ball
  • yellow ball
  • blue ball
  • green cube

Prompt: Sort the blocks into two groups and say the rule you used. Then sort them a different way.

Synthetic assessment demo

Open the test form and pick one invented option. The form is a synthetic test with no real data.

Checking

How to check Google’s confirmation

  1. In the form panel, choose one option and press Google’s Submit button inside the form.
  2. Look for Google’s own confirmation message, which appears inside the form after submitting.
  3. If that confirmation does not appear, treat the response as not submitted.
  4. This page cannot see inside Google’s form, so it never reports whether anything was submitted.
  5. If the embedded form does not load, use Open form in new tab at the bottom of the form panel.
Closing the panel or changing slides does not reload the form, so an unfinished choice stays in place until you submit or leave the page.
