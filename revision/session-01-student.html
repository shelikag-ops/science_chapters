Thinking task · paper only

Thinking task 1: museum groupings

A museum wants to display a dried leaf, a glass bead, a wool thread and a steel nail.

  1. Sketch one way to group them for visitors looking at the display. Write the rule you used.
  2. Sketch a different way to group them for packing them safely in boxes. Write the rule you used.
  3. Explain, in a sentence for each, why each rule suits its purpose.
Paper only. Do not collect these items or physically sort anything.

Written practice · later day

Written practice: how it works

  1. Do it on a different, later day than the reading and thinking tasks. There is no timer.
  2. First pass: answer every question from memory. Leave a question blank if you are not sure.
  3. Then revisit your blanks.
  4. All answers are open responses: a short answer, a paragraph, or a description of a drawing.
